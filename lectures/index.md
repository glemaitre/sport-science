# Sport science notes

Notes to come back to. The first series is cycling physiology, starting from how the muscle makes ATP and then opening the mitochondrion that does most of the work on any ride longer than a minute.

## Cycling physiology

1. [How a cyclist makes energy](cycling-physiology/01-energy-systems.md). The three pathways that remake ATP, why they run together, and how the share changes from a sprint to a road stage.
2. [Inside the mitochondrion](cycling-physiology/02-mitochondria.md). A closer look at the membrane, the proton circuit, the two fuel doors, and the creatine shuttle that connects this machinery to the first lecture.

New notes go in `lectures/` and into the navigation in `mkdocs.yml`. From the project root, `pixi run serve` opens this site at <http://127.0.0.1:8765/sport-science/>, and `pixi run build` writes the static pages into `site/`. The same pages exist in French: use the language switcher, or open `/fr/`.
